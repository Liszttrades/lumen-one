Can I wear glasses with Lumen One?

Yes. Use the glasses spacer for frames up to 142 mm wide, or order snap-in prescription lens inserts.

Do I need a PC or a phone?

No. Lumen One runs on its own. A phone app helps with setup, and a PC is only needed for PC VR games.

How long does the battery last?

About 2.2 hours of typical use. The Pro model's rear battery adds about 30 minutes, and you can play while charging.

What is the warranty?

One year of limited warranty, plus 30 days to return the headset for any reason.

Who can use Lumen One?

Lumen One is for ages 13 and up. Take breaks every 30 minutes and play in a clear space.
